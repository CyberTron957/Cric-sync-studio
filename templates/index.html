Cricket Video Editor

Create synchronized cricket highlight reels with your favorite music

Select your cricket video (MP4, AVI, MOV).
Select a music file (MP3, WAV) to match with video clips. If not provided, original audio will be used.
Import timestamps from a previous session (JSON or TXT file).
How it works:
  1. Upload your cricket video footage and optional music track
  2. Mark timestamps of important moments while watching the video
  3. Our app will create clips synced to the beats of your music or keep original audio
  4. Download your custom cricket highlight reel and optionally the extracted audio
